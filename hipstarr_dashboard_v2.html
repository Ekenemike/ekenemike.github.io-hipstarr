Key Findings — Preliminary
26-track comparative audit · 2019–2024
01
Afrobeats Retains Better
32.4% avg vs 30.6% Latin Pop. Hypothesis disproven. Community depth outperforms market breadth.
02
The Lagos Lock
Lagos is #1 streaming city on 100% of Afrobeats tracks. Nigerian cities average 47–65% of total audience. Structural, not coincidental.
03
Co-sign ≠ Retention
Justin Bieber (6.9M peak) → 17.7% retention. Ed Sheeran → 55.2%. Selena Gomez → 59.5%. Foundation matters more than feature artist scale.
04
The Mexico Anchor
MX is #1 or #2 market on 85% of Latin Pop tracks. The federation distributes across 20+ countries reducing single-market risk.
05
Data-Lifecycle Mismatch
Standard analytics misread both genres. Peak market breadth without city-level geography is a systematically misleading valuation metric.
06
The Organic Premium
Soso: 58.8% retention, 8 editorial playlists. Rush: 29.6%, 35 playlists. Organic slow builds outperform institutional viral drops consistently.
